Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy explains how YTBoostKit handles information when you use our website and free YouTube creator tools.

Last Updated: August 29, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to YTBoostKit. We provide free tools and resources designed to help YouTube creators with content planning, calculations, optimization and workflow.

By using this website, you agree to the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We Collect

Many YTBoostKit tools are designed to work directly in your web browser. Information entered into these tools may be processed locally in your browser and may not be sent to our servers.

Depending on the features used on the website, we may receive limited technical information such as browser type, device information, approximate usage information, pages viewed and other standard website analytics data.

You may voluntarily enter information such as video titles, keywords, topics, descriptions, channel names or other content into our tools.

Unless a particular feature specifically states otherwise, our browser-based tools are designed to process such information locally rather than requiring you to create an account or submit personal information.

Important: Do not enter passwords, payment information, private account credentials or other sensitive personal information into YTBoostKit tools.
