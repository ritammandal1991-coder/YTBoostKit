What Makes a Good YouTube Thumbnail?

A good thumbnail should clearly communicate the video's subject and remain understandable when viewed at a small size. Strong visual hierarchy and readable elements can help viewers quickly understand what they are seeing.

Recommended Thumbnail Size

YouTube thumbnails are commonly created in a 16:9 format. A 1280 × 720 pixel canvas is a practical standard for creating a thumbnail that looks good across different devices.

Does This Tool Predict CTR?

No. This analyzer only performs basic technical and optimization checks. It cannot predict your actual click-through rate or guarantee video performance.
