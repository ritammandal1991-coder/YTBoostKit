What Are YouTube Hashtags?

YouTube hashtags are words or phrases beginning with the # symbol. They can help viewers discover videos related to a particular topic.

How to Use Hashtags

Enter the main topic of your video and generate a list of related hashtags. Review the suggestions and keep only the hashtags that accurately describe your content.

Important Note

Hashtags are only one part of YouTube discovery. Your topic, title, thumbnail, video content and audience response can all influence performance.
