What is YouTube CTR?

YouTube click-through rate (CTR) represents the percentage of counted impressions that resulted in a view. It can help creators understand how often viewers choose to watch a video after seeing its thumbnail and title.

CTR Formula

CTR = (Views from Impressions ÷ Impressions) × 100

Example

If a video receives 100,000 impressions and 5,000 views are attributed to those impressions, the calculated rate is 5%.

Important Note

This calculator provides a simple mathematical estimate. YouTube Analytics may report CTR differently depending on which impressions are counted and how YouTube defines eligible impressions for the metric.
