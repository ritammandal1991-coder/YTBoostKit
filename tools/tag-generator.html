What Are YouTube Video Tags?

Video tags are descriptive keywords that can help provide additional context about your video's content. They are different from hashtags.

How to Use This Tool

Enter your video title, main topic and related keywords. YTBoostKit will generate a list of related tag ideas that you can review and edit before using.

Important Note

Tags are only one part of YouTube discovery. A relevant topic, accurate title, compelling thumbnail and useful video content are also important.
