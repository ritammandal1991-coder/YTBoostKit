What is Engagement Rate?

Engagement rate is a way to compare viewer interactions with the number of views a video receives. This simple calculator combines likes, comments and shares to produce an estimated interaction rate.

Engagement Rate Formula

Engagement Rate = (Likes + Comments + Shares) ÷ Views × 100

Example

Suppose a video receives 100,000 views, 5,000 likes, 300 comments and 200 shares. The estimated interaction rate is 5.5%.

Important Note

This is a simplified calculation created for comparison purposes. YouTube Analytics can contain different engagement-related metrics, definitions and reporting methods.
