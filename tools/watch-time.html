What is YouTube Watch Time?

Watch time is the amount of time viewers spend watching your videos. It is one of the useful metrics creators can monitor when evaluating audience viewing behavior.

Watch Time Formula

Watch Time (hours) = Views × Average View Duration (minutes) ÷ 60

Example

If a video gets 100,000 views and viewers watch an average of 3 minutes per view, the estimated watch time is 5,000 hours.

Important Note

This calculator provides a mathematical estimate. YouTube Analytics may report watch time differently depending on the specific metric, reporting period, traffic source and other factors.
