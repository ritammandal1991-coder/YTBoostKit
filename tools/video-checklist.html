Why Use a YouTube Upload Checklist?

Uploading a video involves more than just pressing the publish button. A simple checklist can help creators review important details before a video goes live.

Review Your Thumbnail and Title

Your title and thumbnail are two of the first things viewers may notice. Make sure both accurately represent your video and are easy to understand.

Don't Forget Accessibility

Captions can make your content easier to understand for more viewers. Review captions before publishing whenever possible.

Final Review

Use this checklist as a final reminder. It is not an official YouTube requirement list, and individual videos may need different settings.
