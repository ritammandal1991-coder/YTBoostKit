What is Subscriber Growth?

Subscriber growth shows how the number of subscribers on a YouTube channel changes over time. This calculator uses a simple average-based projection to estimate a future subscriber count.

Subscriber Growth Formula

New Subscribers = Average Daily Subscribers × Number of Days

Estimated Total = Current Subscribers + New Subscribers

Example

If a channel currently has 10,000 subscribers and gains an average of 100 subscribers per day, the estimated additional subscribers over 30 days would be 3,000. The projected total would therefore be 13,000 subscribers.

Important Note

This is a simple projection, not a prediction. Actual subscriber growth can change significantly because of content performance, audience behavior, seasonality, traffic sources and other factors.
