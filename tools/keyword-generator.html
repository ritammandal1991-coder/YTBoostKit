What Are YouTube Keywords?

Keywords are words or phrases that describe the topic of your video. They can help you plan titles, descriptions and other metadata.

Short-Tail vs Long-Tail Keywords

Short-tail keywords are usually broad terms such as "gardening". Long-tail keywords are more specific phrases such as "gardening tips for beginners".

How to Use This Tool

Enter your main topic and generate keyword ideas. Review the suggestions and select only the phrases that are relevant to your actual video.
